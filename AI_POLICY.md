# AI Policy

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. We ask that you:

- Disclose that AI was used and name the tool/model.
- Review and understand what you submit; you are responsible for it.
- Meet the same quality, testing, and style standards as any contribution.
- Not use fully autonomous agents to open issues or PRs.
- Respond to reviewers yourself.
- Clearly mark AI text in PR descriptions, issues, and comments on PRs/issues.

Using AI for translation or grammar help is fine. Contributions that ignore
this policy may be closed.

Only humans can be named as co-authors, and AI can _never_ sign off on a
commit. The Linux kernel's Assisted-by trailer can be used to credit AI
assistance, like this:

```text
Assisted-by: <harness>:<model>
```

Make sure there are no open PRs already for your contribution. New contributors
are limited to two open PRs at a time, including drafts.

# AI Policy

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. We especially appreciate fixes for bugs
you encounter while using pybind11 and features that help you build something
useful.

## Start with a concrete need

Please explain what motivated your contribution: what problem are you solving,
who benefits, and why does it matter? For a bug fix, include a reproducer or
regression test. For a feature, describe the use case. Discuss substantial
changes with maintainers before investing in an implementation.

Please do not submit issues or PRs primarily to collect contribution credit
or demonstrate an agent's capabilities. Keep experiments whose goal is
automated PR generation in your own repositories. When working from an issue
list, choose a problem whose importance you can explain; avoid submitting
batches of unrelated changes.

## Take responsibility for your contribution

Review your own work and make sure you understand what you submit. Verify
technical claims and meet the project's usual quality, testing, and style
standards. Describe what you tested and note any limitations. When you are
unsure, use AI tools to develop the specific expertise you need to understand
your proposed contribution. Stay involved during review, consider feedback,
and be prepared to explain your choices. Maintainers need an engaged contributor
who takes responsibility throughout the process.

## Be transparent about AI assistance

Disclose AI assistance and name the tool and model used. Briefly describe its
role and how you checked the result. Clearly mark AI-generated text in PR
descriptions, issues, and comments.

Only humans can be named as co-authors. A `Signed-off-by` trailer must represent
a human's own certification. You may credit AI assistance with an optional
trailer:

```text
Assisted-by: <harness>:<model>
```

## Keep review manageable

Maintainer time is limited, and reviewing a contribution can take much longer
than generating it. Please check for existing issues and PRs before submitting,
and focus on the work that matters most.

New contributors may have **at most two open PRs per person in this repository
at a time, including drafts**, whether or not AI was used. If you already have
two open PRs, wait until one is merged or closed before opening another, unless
maintainers explicitly agree otherwise. Focus on completing the review of your
existing PRs.

Maintainers may ask you to narrow or prioritize your work, or close submissions
that do not meet these expectations. A clear use case and thoughtful follow-up
help us make the most of everyone's time.

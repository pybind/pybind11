# AI Policy

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. We especially appreciate fixes for bugs
you encounter while using pybind11 and features that help you build something
useful.

## Start with a concrete need

Please explain the problem or use case that motivated your contribution and
who benefits. Include a reproducer or regression test for bug fixes, and discuss
substantial changes with maintainers before implementation.

Please do not submit issues or PRs primarily to collect contribution credit
or demonstrate an agent's capabilities. Keep experiments in automated PR
generation in your own repositories. When working from an issue list, prioritize
a problem whose importance you can explain; avoid batches of unrelated changes.

## Take responsibility for your contribution

Review and understand what you submit, verify technical claims, and meet the
project's quality, testing, and style standards. Describe your testing and any
limitations. When unsure, use AI tools to build the expertise you need. Stay
involved during review, consider feedback, and be ready to explain your choices.

## Be transparent about AI assistance

Disclose AI assistance and name the tool and model used. Briefly describe its
role and how you checked the result. Clearly mark AI-generated text in PR
descriptions, issues, and comments.

Only humans can be named as co-authors or certify a commit with a `Signed-off-by`
trailer. You may credit AI assistance with:

```text
Assisted-by: <harness>:<model>
```

## Keep review manageable

Reviewing a contribution can take much longer than generating it. Please check
for existing issues and PRs and follow the
[two-PR limit for new contributors](.github/CONTRIBUTING.md#pull-requests).

Maintainers may ask you to narrow or prioritize your work, or close submissions
that do not meet these expectations.
